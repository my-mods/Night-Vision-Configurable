Vampire night vision with a six-metre neutral white-light radius or fullscreen mode. Includes a red vignette toggle and opacity slider, separate 0–100% Black & White and Red monochrome sliders in 5% steps, brightness adjustment, N / hold L3 shortcuts and live Mod Settings controls. Made with permission from opogode, author of Night Vision.

Stays enabled across camera changes and save loading until toggled off or returning to human form, and recovers from camera-effect resets.
